01 · Grover

How can I help Grover?

Worked on Grover's exact problem

At Cashify, used phones were bought, graded, repaired and sold again. We owned the systems that tracked where each device was, what condition it was in and where it went next.

98%stock-in to storage within 7 days, every quarter

Human judgement replaced by system decisions

Grading on our ELSS line was done on paper, by each worker's judgement. We made it rule-based, from defect to spare part to sales channel.

35 → 60devices per person per day

One identity for every device

Every phone got its own life through a barcode number. Once a device entered Cashify's ecosystem, we knew about it, and we built a full fraud layer on top of it.

200 → 0inventory ID errors a month

Partner integrations and migrations

We built Shipex, the layer between our systems and every courier partner, and moved courier allocation off spreadsheets after 2–3 months of running both side by side.

₹1.5 Cr(about €150K) saved in 6 months

Led the ERP integration

We moved Cashify from Tally to Microsoft Dynamics 365. We built an internal layer between our systems and the ERP, locked financial data so no one could edit it by hand, and reconciled old records with backdated systems.

03 · How I work

How I work, and my biggest learnings

  • Spend time with operations. Sometimes the core problem is different from the one people describe.
  • Own the data. Run my own queries and dashboards, so we know what is happening the moment a feature goes live.
  • Keep inventory visible. Inventory is expensive, and every loss meant hours of email chains. We simplified auditing into a flow like courier tracking on Amazon: one scan shows where a device was last seen.
  • Give back to the community. Outside work, I volunteer with The Product Folks, a community for product managers.

I love messy problems and turning them into simple solutions. My motto: build systems that don't break at scale.
